01 — The method

Start with the reader. Then fix the sentence.

Clarity treats writing as a chain of decisions: who needs this, what they need to know, and what only you can say.

01 / Useful

Know who you’re writing for.

Set the reader and the thought they should leave with before polishing the prose.

02 / Clear

Make the claim concrete.

Choose specific words, name the actor and show how ideas connect.

03 / Yours

Keep your point of view.

Cut padding and performance. Keep the details, uncertainty and rhythm that belong to you.

02 — In practice

Less polish.
More point.

Editing should make an idea easier to follow, not make every writer sound the same.
