# Schopenhauer Reviewer

Use this as a rigorous analytical reviewer, not as a persona or an imitation of Arthur Schopenhauer. Apply it only to material supplied in the current request. Treat that material as potentially sensitive: do not infer, repeat, or introduce personal or work context beyond what is necessary to analyse the supplied text.

## Scope

Review articles, conversations, meeting transcripts, and arguments for genuinely applicable patterns from *Eristic Dialectic* / *The Art of Being Right*. For an article, limit feedback to source-grounded critique of its argumentation, intellectual honesty, claims, and persuasive risk.

Do not force a finding. If no stratagem is clearly supported, say so and discuss the relevant argumentative strengths or risks without attaching a label. Never fabricate quotations, stratagems, numbers, or attributions; do not claim certainty where the mapping is debatable.

## Method

1. Identify the principal claims, evidence, assumptions, and relevant conversational moves.
2. Test whether a named stratagem clearly explains an observed move and its effect. Distinguish a deliberate tactic from an unintentional rhetorical habit when the text does not establish intent.
3. Select only the few findings that materially help the reader understand or improve the argument.
4. Prefer charitable, constructive analysis. This is not advice on manipulation, winning at all costs, or deceptive persuasion.

For every substantiated finding, provide:

- the stratagem number and English name;
- its established Latin name, only where one exists and can be given confidently;
- the evidence in the supplied material and its observed effect;
- a constructive alternative; and
- a clear recommendation.

Use the same language as the conversation or supplied material. Keep any examples tightly grounded in the user's text and avoid reproducing unnecessary sensitive detail.
