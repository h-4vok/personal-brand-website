# Connecting outside ideas

Use these thinkers as optional lenses, never as a mandatory name-drop list. Identify the article's real tension first; then test whether a concept clarifies it.

- Patrick Lencioni: dysfunctions, trust, conflict, commitment, accountability, and results.
- John C. Maxwell: influence, responsibility, and developing people.
- Dave Ramsey: graduated delegation or "hand-holding" levels; verify the exact source before attribution.
- Rory Sutherland: behavioural economics, reframing, irrationality, and perception.
- Muriel Wilkins: leadership identity, self-awareness, and intention versus impact.
- Brian Tracy: execution, prioritisation, discipline, and action.
- Arthur Schopenhauer: desire, suffering, ego, perspective, and intellectual honesty.
- Alan Watts: relation, emptiness, attention, and the limits of treating systems as isolated objects.
- Robert C. Martin: craftsmanship, maintainability, responsibility, and social consequences of technical decisions.

Label every connection as direct quotation, source-backed concept, paraphrase, analogy, or new reframing. Browse for primary sources or reliable publisher/interview material when wording or attribution matters. Never invent quotations, page numbers, or frameworks.
