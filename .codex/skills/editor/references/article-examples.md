# Concrete examples

Use the published files in `content/english/blog/` as primary examples. This file is a curated index, not a replacement for reading the source articles. When an approved article adds a durable pattern, add it here with a one-line explanation.

- `2025-08-24-hero-culture-is-a-bug-not-a-feature.md`: an outage story becomes an argument about incentives, resilience, invisible preventive work, data, and shared ownership; it uses three principles.
- `2025-09-21--the-politics-of-tech-debt.md`: reframes technical debt as a shared business and political problem, then gives leaders a toolkit.
- `2025-12-28--vendor-selection-horror-story.md`: uses failure, ignored red flags, a phased method, and a conclusion about courage and method agnosticism.
- `2026-02-17--bring-your-own-solution.md`: challenges a management slogan and uses levels of leadership involvement to discuss delegation and psychological safety.
- `2026-06-20--the-profitability-of-void-space.md`: turns "space" into a leadership concept and challenges busyness and meeting overload.
- `2026-04-14--agentic-ai-escaping-attention-trap.md`: combines a personal build story with the human boundary that AI supports attention but does not speak for the author.

Draft seeds are less polished. `deep-meeting` contains a conflict-observation sequence (breathe, think, listen); `from-jr-to-sr` points to Dave Ramsey's three hand-holding levels; `leadership-stances` points to an HBR concept; `template` develops void, autonomy, busyness, and Alan Watts; `diagram-tours` and `umami-article-depth` are product/content ideas.
